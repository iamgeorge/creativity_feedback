Instructions

Your mission

You'll be given a common, everyday object. Think of as many different, unusual, and creative uses for it as you can. The more creative and original, the better!

Setup

  • Read the object name at the top of the screen
  • You have 5 minutes for each object
  • Give at least 3 answers within the 5 minutes to move on
  • The timer at the top of the screen shows the time left. Reminders are also announced at 1 minute, at 30 seconds, and when time is up

How it works

  1. Type one use in the answer box
  2. Click “Get Feedback” and read the feedback
  3. Click “Add Another Answer” for your next idea
  4. Keep going until time’s up

Meaningful answers only

Every answer should be a real use for the object. Random letters or filler such as “idk” get no feedback and do not count toward your 3 answers. If that happens, change the answer to a real idea and click “Get Feedback” again.

Remember: there are no right answers, just creative ones!

Your mission

You'll see an abstract, unfinished figure. Imagine what it could become, then draw on it to turn it into a picture. The more unusual and creative, the better!

Setup

  • Study the figure
  • You have 5 minutes for each figure
  • Submit at least 3 drawings within the 5 minutes to move on
  • The timer at the top of the screen shows the time left. Reminders are also announced at 1 minute, at 30 seconds, and when time is up

How it works

  1. Draw on the figure with your mouse, finger, or stylus (click “Clear” to start over)
  2. Click “Submit Drawing” when you’re done
  3. Read the feedback
  4. Click “New Drawing” to start another drawing on the same figure
  5. Make as many different drawings as you can until time’s up

Remember: there are no right answers, just creative ones!
