Creativity Study

Thanks for joining our creativity study

Three parts, about 65 minutes, done in one sitting. Complete all three to receive your gift card.

The three parts, in order

  1. Pre-survey about 10 minutes

    Quick background questions before the task.

  2. Creativity tasks about 50 minutes

    10 short tasks, 5 minutes each. This is where the fun begins!

  3. Post-survey about 5 minutes

    Reflection questions after finishing the task.

What to expect

Total time
About 65 minutes
Compensation
A gift card when you finish (yes, you get paid to be creative!)
Format
Online, at your convenience, through a unique link sent to your email
Requirements
Signed informed consent (at the start of the pre-survey) and all three parts completed

Before you start

  • Complete all three parts to get paid
  • Find your creative zone: a quiet space with no interruptions
  • Stay connected: a stable internet connection is a must
  • Block out 65 minutes and do it all in one sitting
  • Be yourself and have fun: this is NOT a test! We want YOUR unique ideas.

Questions before, during, or after the study? Contact the research team at ashley.shen@unt.edu.
